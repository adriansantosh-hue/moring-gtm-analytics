# Moring AI — Website Positioning Rewrite (Homepage + Key Pages)

**Date:** 2026-09-01. **Purpose:** fix the message-to-site disconnect — the research says own "Governed Business Workflow Agents," but the site leads with "Enterprise AI Platform / AI Control Plane." This is the rewrite blueprint (Webflow, no dev needed).

**The core reframe (from Part 9B):**
> **Old:** "Moring AICP — The Control Plane for Enterprise AI." (platform-first, competes head-on with ServiceNow/MS/IBM/Guild on a word they all own)
> **New:** "Moring delivers governed AI for the business workflows you actually run — claims, KYC, prior-auth, payments — in your cloud, outcome-priced, with the audit trail your regulators accept." (workflow-first, the unoccupied position)

## HOMEPAGE — the rewrite

**H1 (hero):**
> **Governed AI that gets your workflows to production — not stuck at the demo.**

**Subhead (the one-liner):**
> Moring delivers governed AI agents for the workflows your business actually runs — claims, KYC, prior-auth, payments, operations. In your cloud. Outcome-priced. With the audit trail your CISO and regulators accept.

**Hero CTA (one, not three):**
> Start with a 30-minute AI Discovery Workshop → [button]

**The proof strip (numbers that win):**
- 99.9% uptime SLA · <200ms latency · ~40% cost savings · 14 days to live · 78% of Fortune 500 use AI but only 22% orchestrate 3+ agents (the gap)

**Problem section (name the pain, don't sell the platform):**
> "Your AI is stuck at the demo. Building, running, and governing are split across three teams. Developers can't get past the pilot. Platform teams hand out parts that don't fit. Security won't scale what it can't see. **Gartner: >40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by 2027.** Moring is the one place governance, delivery, and a measured outcome come together."

**What we deliver (workflow-first, not platform-first):**
1. **Governed Business Workflow Agents** — claims preparation, KYC/onboarding evidence, prior-auth routing, payment exceptions. *We deliver the agents, you keep the decision.*
2. **AI-DLC** — governed agentic software delivery (per-PR attribution, failure drill).
3. **AI Ops** — the governed operational brain for mission-critical ops.
> *(All three run on one governed substrate — Moring AICP — but the workflow is the door, not the platform.)*

**The 4 principles (keep, already right):**
1. Bespoke, not boxed · 2. Governed underneath · 3. Outcome-priced · 4. Knowledge transferred (no lock-in).

**"Where Moring wins" block (the defensibility — new):**
> **Why Moring, not the platform giants:** ServiceNow, Microsoft, and IBM all claim a "control plane" — but they're platforms you're locked into, or policy registries that only watch. **Moring delivers governed agents in YOUR cloud, to YOUR regulators' rules, tied to a measured outcome, with the evidence your CISO accepts.** Weeks not years. No lock-in.
> **Why Moring, not DIY:** open source gets you the pilot. Moring gets you past the CISO. The governance wall (audit, PII, surprise API bills, approval fatigue) is where pilots die — build on a governed substrate we deliver instead.

**The proof anchor:**
> **Forrester named the agent control plane a market.** "Oversight must live outside the agent's execution loop." Moring delivers all five control-plane capabilities — with delivered workflow agents, not just a dashboard.

**Footer language:** keep solutions/industries/workshops — but reorder to lead with **Industries (Insurance, Fintech & Banks)** then Solutions (AI-DLC, AI Ops, Moring AICP), matching the workflow-first order.
