# Moring AI — Revenue Targets by Segment (Enterprise + Mid-Market) with Timing

**Date:** 2026-09-01. Scenario model grounded in the combined revenue model + live pipeline data (2026-08-31). Enterprise and mid-market have different ACV, cycle, and motion — they must be modeled separately and run in parallel.

---

## 1. The Two-Track Model (Enterprise + Mid-Market, parallel)

| Variable | Mid-Market | Enterprise | Why different |
|---|---|---|---|
| Segment size (US) | 500–5,000 emp; ~800-1,200 eligible accounts | 5,000+ emp; ~300-500 eligible accounts | TAM/SAM doc |
| Entry ACV | **$200K** | **$350–400K** (platform-anchored, higher scope) | Enterprise needs AICP + more control/evidence |
| Post-expansion ACV | **$400K** | **$750K–1M** | Enterprise expands across BUs |
| Sales cycle | **2–4 months** (Type A + B + light C) | **6–9 months** (full committee + procurement) | BCG/Procurement research |
| POC→deal | 40–50% | 25–35% | Regulated-enterprise stalls at internal approval |
| Motion | Sales-led + workshop, founder-closeable | Sales-led + ABM + AWS co-sell + SI partner | Can't cold-close enterprise at $350K |
| Buyer path | Type B sponsor → Type A funds → Type C approves | Multi-threaded A/B/C + architecture + procurement | Enterprise = committee |
| Channel | Direct + light partner | **AWS co-sell + SI partner (mandatory)** | Enterprise reach needs distribution |
| Time-to-first-meeting | 2–4 weeks | 4–8 weeks | Committee + warm intro needed |

**The honest combined numbers:**

This is **more ambitious than the prior $5–8M→$10–15M** doc because it explicitly models the two segments as parallel tracks — mid-market funds cash flow while enterprise builds the higher-ACV base. Both are needed; neither alone hits the target.

## 5. What Makes It Real (the levers per track)

**Mid-market levers (speed):**
1. Founder + 1 AE closing (8–12 deals/yr each).
2. Outcome-priced wedge → creditable POC → expand on same substrate.
3. The live outbound engine (email 21.9% LinkedIn reply already) scaled with fresh signals + campaign pages.

**Enterprise levers (scale):**
1. **AWS co-sell + Marketplace** (fix the false claim; list now) — enterprise procurement fast-lane.
2. **SI partner program** — 3–5 partners Y1 → 10+ Y2 feeding enterprise accounts.
3. **Named-account ABM** — the A/B/C committee multi-thread already running (3.7 DMs/account).
4. **AICP platform-anchored pricing** — $400K+ entry + $750K–1M expansion.

**Gating factors (honest):**
- **Delivery capacity is the bottleneck, not sales** — FDEs must scale with deals (cash to fund delivery before revenue lands).
- **Enterprise cycle (6–9 months)** — don't count enterprise in Y1 cash; it's a Y2-Y3 payoff.
- **Partner quality** — 10 productive SIs, not 10 logos.
- **POC→deal conversion** (mid 40-50%, ent 25-35%) — regulated buyers stall at internal approval; win Type C early.
- **SR 26-2 RFI + EU AI Act (Aug 2 2026) timing** — hit the "why now" while hot.

- **Weeks 0-2:** Fix AWS claim + list · campaign pages (C1 KYC) · refresh 20 stale signals · rebalance ICP Type C/A.
- **Weeks 3-4:** C1 opens · founder-LinkedIn (Balaji) · data report (GEO) · **land first 1-2 mid-market workshops → POCs.**
- **Weeks 5-8:** Convert first POCs → **first paying mid-market deals (Q3)** · C2 launches · backfill emails.

**Target by end of 12 weeks:** 2–4 mid-market POCs live, 1–2 converted to revenue, 1–2 enterprise POCs in motion, 5 meetings/week.
