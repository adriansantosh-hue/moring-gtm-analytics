# Moring AI — Combined Revenue Model (All Levers Together)

Date: 21 Aug 2026. Scenario model, not a guarantee. Base = $200K ACV wedge deals.

---

## 0. The Core Insight

**$3M is the ceiling of the "current model" (single-founder, cold outbound, $200K wedge deals).**
To go past it, you change the model, not just work harder. **Four levers, stacked together:**

1. **Land-and-expand ACV** — $200K → $400K+ per account (one substrate, compounding).
2. **Partner/SI channel at scale** — 10 SIs bringing accounts you can't reach cold.
3. **Platform-anchored pricing** — sell AICP (enterprise-wide) as the anchor, solutions as expansion.
4. **Sales team capacity** — more AEs = more deals (1 AE closes ~8–12/yr).

## 2. Combined 3-Year Revenue Build

### Year 1 — Foundation (direct, entry deals)
- 1 AE + founder closing; build list, motion, first proof.
- **Deals:** ~8–12 entry deals ($200K).
- **ARR:** **$1.5–2.4M.**

### Year 2 — Land-and-expand + first partners (the jump)
- Existing Y1 accounts **expand** $200K → $400K avg (+$1.6–2.4M from expansion alone).
- **Add ~4–6 new deals** from first 3–5 partners.
- **2 AEs** now closing.
- **ARR:** **$5–7M.**

### Year 3 — Platform pricing + full partner channel + team (scale)
- AICP platform-anchored deals price **$400K–$1M**.
- **10 partners × 2–3 deals × $300K+** = **+$6–9M** channel revenue.
- **3 AEs + FDEs** delivering.
- Existing accounts continue expanding.
- **ARR:** **$10–15M.**

## 5. The Honest Gating Factors (what could stop it)

- **Team & delivery capacity:** $10–15M needs 3+ AEs + enough FDEs to deliver 30–45 deals. Delivery is the bottleneck, not sales.
- **Partner recruitment quality:** 10 productive SIs (not 10 logos). Each must be able to sell + deliver governed agents.
- **ACV expansion rate:** land-and-expand only works if the first wedge delivers real outcome + the substrate really compounds (cross-sell AI-DLC → AI Ops → business agents).
- **Market/regulatory timing:** SR 26-2 RFI + Gartner 40%-cancellation are tailwinds that decay; hit them while hot.
- **POC→deal conversion (30–50%):** regulated buyers stall on internal approvals even after a good POC.
- **Cash to fund FDEs before revenue lands:** you spend delivery cost before you invoice.

## 6. The One-Line Answer

**Yes — $3M is low if you pull all four levers.**
- Current model (alone): **$1.5–2.4M Y1 → ~$3M.**
- **Everything together: ~$2M Y1 → $5–7M Y2 → $10–15M Y3** (~30–45 accounts + channel, $400–600K avg ACV).

**The ceiling isn't your effort — it's ACV expansion + partner channel + team capacity.** Pull those and the number stops feeling low.

## 7. To Get There, Do These in Order

1. **Y1:** Nail the wedge motion + land 8–12 entry deals + deliver real outcomes (so expansion is provable). Start recruiting 3–5 partners.
2. **Y2:** Aggressively expand existing accounts (cross-sell on the substrate); add a 2nd AE + FDEs; grow partners to 5+; start pricing AICP as the anchor.
3. **Y3:** Full partner channel (10+) + platform-anchored pricing + 3 AEs → **$10–15M ARR.**
