## 1. What We Sell (portfolio)

1. **AI Control Plane (AICP)** — the governance/control layer: auth/authz (OPA/Cedar), AI gateway, MCP platform, skills registry, pattern library/blueprints, governance workflow engine, secure HITL, SME tooling, agent runtime, agent registry, cost controls. Runs in customer's cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP/on-prem), never hosted. SLAs: 99.9% uptime, <200ms, ~40% cost savings.
2. **AI-DLC** — governed agentic SDLC. 5 components: cost observability, governed workflow, plugin distribution, failure drill, 5-engineer case study. $5–10M Y1 (eng-only, 200 engineers). 14-day path.
3. **Agentic Solutions** — AI Ops (correlation/RCA/self-healing), AI Finance (fraud/AML/KYC/payments), AI Customer Support (claims/warranty/service), AI Software Engineering (code/test/refactor).
   - AI Ops: −70–85% alert noise, −50–70% MTTR, catch 7–60 min early.
   - Insurance: claims triage −40% cycle; NAIC/MRM governed.

**Pricing model:** platform fee (AICP) + per-solution fee; outcome-priced (fee triggers only if outcome ships). Paid creditable POC. **Cost/price sized to company + scope** (not a fixed list price). Target ~$200K entry ACV, expand to $400K+ via land-and-expand.

## 9. The Revenue Model (the money)

### Honest framing
- **$3M is the ceiling of the "current model"** (solo + cold outbound + $200K wedge deals). 8–15 deals × $200K = $1.6–3M.
- To go past it, **change the model** — pull 4 levers: (1) land-and-expand ACV, (2) partner/SI channel, (3) platform-anchored pricing, (4) sales team capacity.

### The one-year scale (all levers, front-loaded)

**Non-negotiables from month 1:** 3–5 partners already signed · entry+expansion priced up-front ($300–400K) · you + 1–2 AEs + FDEs · warm network + AWS co-sell active.

| Q1 | 2–3 | $250K | $0.6M |
| Q2 | 5–6 | $300K | $1.8M |
| Q3 | 8–10 | $350K | $4.2M |

### The 3-year combined trajectory (everything together)

```
Year          Y1          Y2          Y3
ARR         $2-8M      $5-7M      $10-15M
Deals/yr    8-12       14-20       30-45
Avg ACV     $200-400K  $300-400K   $400-600K
AEs          1-2         2-3         3-4
Partners    starts      3-5        10+
```

**Realistic one-year: $5–8M ARR** (needs ~20 accounts at ~$400K + channel + a team). **$10–15M by Y3.**

### Honest gating factors
- **Delivery/FDE capacity is the real bottleneck** (not sales).
- **Partner quality** (producers, not logos).
- **ACV expansion rate** (does the first wedge deliver + does the substrate really compound).
- **POC→deal conversion 30–50%** (regulated buyers stall on internal approvals).
- **Cash flow** — you spend delivery cost before you invoice (outcome-pricing + FDE).
- **Market/regulatory timing** decays (SR 26-2 RFI, Gartner 40%-cancellation).
