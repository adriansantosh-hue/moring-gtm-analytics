# Moring AI — ABM Multi-Touch Sequence Playbook

Date: 21 Aug 2026. Part of the Moring GTM doc set (`Moring_AI_GTM_Playbook.md` + others).
Goal: land AI Discovery Workshops from named, decision-maker-led ABM accounts.

---

## 0. The ABM Principle

**Fewer, deeper named accounts — not broad-and-shallow.** For a ~$200K product with a multi-person buying committee, **depth beats volume**.

- **~500–1,000 named ABM accounts** (US regulated ICP: banking/insurance/healthcare/mission-critical/engineering/payments; 500–5,000 or strategic 5,000+).
- **3–5 decision-makers per account** (Type A/B/C) + 2–4 influencers.
- **~14–15 touches per account** across LinkedIn + email + content.
- **~7,000–14,000 touchpoints total**, orchestrated by Clay + Instantly + HeyReach + ABM ads.

**Scale levers (not just raw account count):**
1. Add more named accounts (500 → 1,000+).
2. Touch more people *inside* each account (3–5 DMs → 8–10 incl. influencers).
3. **Partners/SIs + AWS co-sell + referrals** — the uncapped multiplier that brings their own accounts.

## 2. The Multi-Touch Sequence (full orchestration)

**Objective:** get the whole committee to an AI Discovery Workshop.
**3 channels × ~4–5 touches each**, layered and role-personalized, ~5 weeks.

### WEEKS 1–2 — Research & Prep (Clay)
- Find all 3–5 decision-makers + influencers.
- Capture signals (hiring, stack, regulatory, content, budget).
- Draft role-specific angles.

---

### WEEK 1 — LinkedIn Opens (HeyReach) — connect only, no pitch

| Touch | Channel | Target | Message angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | LinkedIn connect | **Type B** (champion) | Personal, signal-specific ("saw your team building agents…") |
| L2 | LinkedIn connect | **Type A** (CAIO/CIO) | Outcome / board pressure ("get out of the pilot") |
| L3 | LinkedIn connect | **Type C** (CISO/model-risk) | Governance / SR 26-2 ("survive the review") |

---

### WEEKS 2–4 — Email Sequence (Instantly) — 4-touch, staggered, one angle each

| Touch | When | Target | Email angle |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Day 1 | Type B | **Problem**: "AI stuck at demo / can't hire talent — we plug in, finish fast" + offer workshop |
| E2 | Day 4 | Type A | **Outcome**: baseline + yes/no on outcome pricing, 30-min, low commitment |
| E3 | Day 7 | Type C | **Governance**: policy-at-substrate, audit lake, SR 26-2/NAIC/HIPAA, SOC 2 |
| E4 | Day 11 | All | **Proof**: one strong number (78%→31%, $5–10M, −70–85% noise) + featured-on-[show] / clip |

*(One person per email, but the account receives 4 emails — the committee sees you repeatedly.)*

---

### WEEKS 2–4 — LinkedIn Follow-up (HeyReach) — in parallel

| Touch | When | Message angle |
|---|---|---|
| L4 | after connect | Warm nudge to the workshop |
| L5 | if silent | Share a clip / ROI calc — "thought you'd want this" (no ask) |
| L6 | if 2+ engaged | Ask: "happy to run this for your [CTO + CISO] — who's the right person?" |

---

### WEEKS 2–5 — Content Layer (always-on, for non-repliers)

| Touch | Channel | Action |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | ABM display / LinkedIn matched-audience | Serve **gated whitepaper** to all DMs |
| C2 | Same | Serve **POC-to-Production ROI calculator** |
| C3 | Same | Serve **industry one-pager** (insurance/banking/healthcare) |

*(Even silent committee members see you ~3× in the side-channel.)*

---

### WEEK 4+ — Trigger-Based Escalation

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| Any DM clicks/reads/opens whitepaper | Immediate personalized email + LinkedIn nudge (AE takes over) |
| 2+ stakeholders engage | Offer **Discovery Workshop for the whole committee** |
| Committee accepts | **BOOK THE MEETING** — baseline + architecture sketch + yes/no on outcome pricing |
| No reply after ~12–14 touches | Stop / move to nurture bucket (re-engage in 30–60 days on a new signal) |

## 3. Full Touch Count Per Account

- ~3 LinkedIn connects + 3 follow-ups = **6 LinkedIn**
- 4 emails = **4 email**
- ~3 content/ABM views = **3 content**
- ~2 trigger escalations = **2**

**≈ 14–15 touches** per account across 3 channels, 4–5 people, ~5 weeks.

```
Research → L1·L2·L3 (connect)
```

## 5. Role-Specific Messaging (the angle bank)

### Type A — Strategic Outcome Buyer (CAIO/CDO/CIO/COO)
**Pain:** board pressure, no production agents, time-to-value, budget fear.
- "Get out of the pilot and into production — in weeks, not years."
- "Baseline first, verify second, bill third — we only get paid if the outcome ships."
- "78% of the Fortune 500 use AI, but only 31% have a single agent in production."
- "Gartner: >40% of agentic projects will be canceled by 2027. Don't waste the budget."
- **Ask:** 30-min workshop for a baseline metric + yes/no on outcome pricing.

### Type B — Operating Workflow Buyer (VP Eng/DevEx/Ops/Claims/Fraud)
**Pain:** cycle time, MTTR, alert noise, adoption, can't hire AI talent.
- "Your AI is stuck at the demo because hiring, building, and governing take forever. We plug in and finish fast."
- "AI-DLC: $5–10M Y1 impact; per-PR cost attribution; your platform team owns the source by day 12."
- "AI Ops: −70–85% alert noise, −50–70% MTTR, catch incidents 7–60 min early."
- "We embed an engineer with your SMEs and hand over the outcome — no lock-in."
- **Ask:** workshop on your workflow + one outcome metric.

### Type C — Control & Technical Buyer (CISO/CRO/Head of AI Gov/Model Risk)
**Pain:** audit pass, model risk, SR 26-2, governance, security review.
- "Policy at the substrate (OPA/Cedar), not in the prompt."
- "MCP scoping, agent registry + kill switch, audit lake with trace-ID forensics (<1 min retrieval)."
- "SR 26-2, NAIC, MRM, FFIEC, HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001 patterns baked in."
- "Deployed in your VPC — zero data exfiltration, your keys, your audit trail."
- "Forrester: oversight must live outside the agent's execution loop — Moring delivers all five control-plane capabilities."
- **Ask:** architecture review / workshop for the governance evidence.

### Influencers (engineers, analysts, directors)
- Share the **ROI calculator**, **whitepaper**, **open-source recipe**, **clips**.
- Enlist them to bring the committee together ("who's the right person?").

## 6. Channels & Touch Rules (recap)

- **LinkedIn (HeyReach):** connect + personalized follow-ups. Never pitch in connect.
- **Email (Instantly):** 4-touch sequence, one angle per touch, verified work emails, sender-health managed, reply-centralized.
- **Content (ABM ads):** always-on whitepaper / ROI calc / industry one-pager to the matched-audience of each account's DMs.
- **Trigger:** any engagement → escalate to AE immediately.
- **Operating rule:** Clay = who/why/route. Instantly = approved email. HeyReach = approved LinkedIn. Everything reviewed before send (compliance-reviewed for EU regions).

## 7. Scaling — Adding More Companies

**You can add more than 1,000 — three ways:**
1. **More named accounts** (500 → 1,000 → 1,500+): the limit is executable touches, not companies. Clay + Instantly + HeyReach comfortably run 500–1,000 at full multi-touch depth.
2. **More people per account** (3–5 DMs → 8–10 incl. influencers): grows coverage without adding companies.
3. **Partners/SIs + AWS co-sell + referrals** (uncapped): each active partner brings their own accounts — **the biggest scale lever**.
