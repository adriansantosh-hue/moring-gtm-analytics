## THE THREE AXES NOBODY ELSE OWNS

Competitors can copy any single play. None can copy all three at once. **Moring wins where these three intersect:**

### 1. Regulated Business Workflows (not engineering estates, not policy registries)
- **Who owns what:** Guild owns engineering-agent estates · Devin owns coding · Credo/IBM own policy/GRC · Duck Creek owns insurance-core-native · **Moring owns the regulated BUSINESS workflow** (claims, KYC, prior-auth, payments, ops).
- **The defensible proof:** SR 26-2, NAIC/MRM, HIPAA, FFIEC, SOC 2 compliance mapping + a trace-ID audit lake + human-in-the-loop approval on the workflow itself. No competitor ships this depth on regulated workflow evidence.

### 2. Delivered Agents + Forward-Deployed Engineering (not frameworks, not platforms you build on)
- **Who owns what:** LangChain/Lyzr are frameworks you build on · ServiceNow/MS are platforms you compose inside · **Moring delivers the governed agents** (claims prep, KYC evidence, prior-auth routing) with an FDE embedded in your stack.
- **The defensible proof:** "We deliver the agent, you keep the decision" — the anti-build-project, anti-implementation-burden position. IBM (G2: steep learning curve) and every framework lose this.

### 3. Outcome-Priced, In-Your-Cloud, No-Lock-In (not per-seat/consumption taxes)
- **Who owns what:** MS A365 is per-seat + consumption ($3-5k/mo at 100 seats) · ServiceNow is metered per-action (~$150/agentic action) · Devin is seat+usage · **Moring prices a fixed outcome** — the fee only triggers if the measured outcome ships.
- **The defensible proof:** this directly answers G2's #1 buyer complaint (33% unpredictable pricing) + the Reddit "surprise API bill" killer + Sinch's "guardrail tax" (84% of eng time on safety infra). And "in your cloud, you own the keys" kills the lock-in objection every incumbent carries.
