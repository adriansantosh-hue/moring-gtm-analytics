# Tagging protocol

- Max two company tags in a post body. Other tools appear as plain names or in the first comment line "Works with: ...".
- Tag when the post teaches something about the product or credits its work (docs, standards). Never to ask for a share.
- No competitor tags. No regulator tags (OCC, Fed, FDIC, NAIC are named, not tagged, unless crediting a published resource).
- Never imply endorsement or partnership. Moring has none with these companies.
- Verify the real company page first (wrong-entity bleed is a known risk). Check X handles; never guess.
- People: tag only after prior contact and consent.
- Log every tag (post, page tagged, any reaction) in the engagement loop sheet.
- Mentions can be switched off by members, and LinkedIn may limit mentions of non-connections (LinkedIn Help: "Mention people in your posts").
