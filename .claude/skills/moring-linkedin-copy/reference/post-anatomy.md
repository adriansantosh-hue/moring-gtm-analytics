# Post anatomy (LinkedIn)

Length: 775 to 1,318 characters worked well; hard limit 3,000. The first 2 lines show before "see more", so they carry the hook.

1. **Hook (1 to 2 lines).** A question or a specific number with its source. No "excited to share".
2. **The problem in 2 to 3 sentences.** A concrete failure, not a category.
3. **Numbered steps (3 to 5).** Each step = what to do + a probe + a pass sign.
4. **How to score or decide.** A threshold or rule of thumb the reader can apply.
5. **Human line.** Who decides, who approves.
6. **Free file line.** "Free file, no form. Link in the comments."
7. **Closing question.** For practitioners, specific, answerable in one line.

X version: under 280 characters, the hook plus the count ("12 probes, free file"), arrow down. The X reply carries the asset link.

First comment template:
`Free file, no form: [ASSET_URL]. Longer read: [ARTICLE_URL]. Works with: [plain tool names]. #hashtag #hashtag`

Visual: GIF from moring-gif-studio; alt text is the target keyword question; no URL inside the GIF.

## Good (T1-P1 hook)
"Do you have an AI control plane, or a dashboard?" then the three questions with probes and pass signs.

## Avoid
- Gated PDFs as the value; teasers that withhold the content.
- "Book a demo", "let's talk", any meeting ask.
- Stats without source and year; Moring numbers not labeled proposed/modeled/measured.
- Tag clusters, hashtag walls, links in the body.
