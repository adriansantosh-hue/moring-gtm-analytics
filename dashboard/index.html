Messaging Change Strategy

Previous approach
"Moring provides a governed AI control plane with identity, permissions, policy enforcement, and audit evidence."
Proposed approach
"When clients run AI agents, they need to know what the AI accessed, what it did, and who approved it."
New language
Logs, tickets, spreadsheets, actions, approvals, reports, human decisions
Low-risk offer
One workflow, 15-minute conversation, free pilot, payment only if agreed value delivered

Why This Change Works

  1. Recognizable problem
    "What did the AI do, and who approved it?" - prospect immediately understands
  2. Avoids unfamiliar terms
    No need to understand AICP, control planes, or AI-governance architecture
  3. Operational language
    Logs, tickets, spreadsheets - familiar to business and technical buyers
  4. Insight over logos
    Demonstrates credibility by accurately describing buyer's operational problem
